      <h2>English</h2>
      <p>Goose Rise is the game studio of Cem Tokgöz, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). This policy applies to every Goose Rise game, wherever you play it: on web game portals (such as Poki), on Google Play and on the App Store. If a store listing says a game does something different, the store listing and this policy are read together.</p>

      <h3>1. What our games collect</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Game progress, on your device only.</strong> Levels, stars, points and settings are saved in your browser or in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us. Clearing site data or uninstalling the game deletes them.</li>
        <li><strong>Gameplay analytics.</strong> Events such as "level 3 started", "level 3 completed" or "settings changed", together with technical data the analytics service collects automatically: device model, operating system, app version, language, country or region (derived from the IP address) and a random app-instance identifier. We use it to find bugs and balance levels. It is not linked to your name or email.</li>
        <li><strong>Crash reports</strong> (mobile apps): the state of the app when it crashed, device model and operating system.</li>
        <li><strong>Advertising data, only in games that show ads.</strong> The ad provider may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and device information to show, cap and measure ads. Personalised ads are shown only where you have given consent where that is required (for example the consent prompt in the EU/UK, or App Tracking Transparency on iOS).</li>
      </ul>
      <p>We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. Our games have no accounts, no chat and no user-generated content. If a game ever offers purchases, the store handles the payment and we never see your payment details.</p>

      <h3>2. Services we use, by platform</h3>

      <h3>3. Why we use data</h3>
      <p>To run the games, fix crashes, understand which levels are too hard or too easy, and keep the games free with ads. Under GDPR and KVKK our legal basis is our legitimate interest for analytics and crash reports, and your consent for personalised ads where consent is required.</p>

      <h3>4. How long we keep it</h3>
      <p>Analytics events are kept for no longer than 14 months and crash reports for no longer than 90 days, then deleted by the provider. Your local save stays on your device until you delete it.</p>

      <h3>5. Children</h3>
      <p>Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us, write to us and we will delete it. Portals that serve children apply their own child-safe settings, such as non-personalised ads.</p>

      <h3>6. Your choices and rights</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings, and turn off personalised ads in the consent prompt or in your Google / Apple settings.</li>
        <li>Clear the game's site data or uninstall the app to delete your local save.</li>
        <li>Ask us to access or delete data linked to your device, or exercise any right you have under KVKK, GDPR, UK law or California law: email us and we will answer within 30 days.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>We do not sell personal data.</p>

      <h3>7. Changes and contact</h3>
