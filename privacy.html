English

This policy explains what data Kicksort ("the game") handles and how. It applies to the web version published on Poki and to the Android and iOS apps.

1. Who we are

Kicksort is developed by Goose Rise, an independent game studio based in Türkiye.

Company: {{SIRKET_UNVANI}}, {{ADRES}}.

2. Data we collect

  • Anonymous gameplay analytics. The game sends events such as "level 3 started", "level 3 completed", "balls left: 2" or "first curve shot: shot 4". These events contain no name, email, account, device identifier or location; they are not linked to you as a person. We use them only to balance levels and improve the game.
  • Progress saved on your device. Your level, stars, points, equipped ball and settings are stored in your browser's local storage or in the app's local storage. They never leave your device and are deleted when you clear the site data or uninstall the app.

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases. There is no way to enter personal information in the game.

3. Web version on Poki

When you play on poki.com (or a site that embeds Poki games), the Poki platform serves the advertisements and processes the anonymous gameplay events through its own infrastructure. Cookies, consent choices and ad personalisation on Poki are governed by Poki's rules; see the Poki Privacy Center.

4. Mobile apps (Android / iOS)

The apps contain no Poki code and show no Poki advertisements. The same anonymous gameplay events may be sent to an analytics provider (for example Google Firebase Analytics) so we can see how levels perform; they contain no personal identifiers. If the apps ever add advertising or any new data processing, this policy and its date will be updated first.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We have none to sell.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game is suitable for all ages.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete; your local save can be removed by clearing site data or uninstalling the app. If you believe we do hold personal data about you, or you have any question, write to us and we will answer within 30 days. Players in the EU/EEA, the UK, Türkiye (KVKK), California and elsewhere keep all rights granted by their local law.

7. Changes

We will update this page and the date above before any change in what the game collects takes effect.

2. Topladığımız veriler

  • Anonim oyun içi analitik olayları. Oyun; "3. bölüm başladı", "3. bölüm tamamlandı", "kalan top: 2", "ilk falsolu şut: 4. atış" gibi olaylar gönderir. Oyunda hesap, giriş, sohbet, kullanıcı içeriği veya uygulama içi satın alma yoktur. Oyuna kişisel bilgi girilebilecek bir alan bulunmaz.

Aynı anonim oyun olayları, bölümlerin nasıl oynandığını görebilmemiz için bir analitik sağlayıcısına (örneğin Google Firebase Analytics) gönderilebilir; kişisel tanımlayıcı içermez. Uygulamalara reklam veya yeni bir veri işleme eklenirse önce bu politika ve tarihi güncellenir.
