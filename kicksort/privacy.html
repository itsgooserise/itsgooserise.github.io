Kick Sort! Privacy Policy

Goose Rise · Effective 9 October 2026

Personal data

Kick Sort! does not collect personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no way to enter your name, email or any other personal information.

Saved on your device

Your progress and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are removed when you clear your browser's site data.

Ads and statistics

The game runs on the Poki platform. Poki serves the ads and receives anonymous gameplay events (such as a level being started or completed) that contain no personal data. Cookies and ad choices are managed by Poki; see the Poki Privacy Center.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the game starts collecting any data, this page and its effective date will be updated first.
